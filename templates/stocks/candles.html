What is a Candlestick?

A candlestick shows 4 key prices in one glance — Open, High, Low, Close. Click each part of the candle to learn what it means.

🟢 Green / Bullish
Close > Open
Buyers won the session
Positive day
🔴 Red / Bearish
Close < Open
Sellers won the session
Negative day
Body
The thick rectangle between Open and Close. Green body = Close above Open (bullish). Red body = Close below Open (bearish). A larger body shows stronger conviction.
Upper Wick / Shadow
The thin line above the body reaching up to the HIGH. A long upper wick means sellers rejected higher prices — they pushed it down from the high.
Lower Wick / Shadow
The thin line below the body reaching down to the LOW. A long lower wick means buyers defended lower prices — they pushed it back up from the low.
Open Price
Where price started the time period. For a green candle, the open is the bottom of the body. For a red candle, the open is the top of the body.
Close Price
Where price ended the time period. The most important price of the candle — it reflects the final verdict of buyers vs sellers for that session.
